# Zameel v1.3.8 – Graduation Book Spread Redesign

- Each graduate/member owns exactly two pages in a spread.
- Left page is reserved for photos and memories.
- Right page is reserved for handwritten notes, movable text, and stickers.
- Text and stickers support move, scale, rotate, and long-press delete.
- Handwriting supports pen, eraser (last stroke), undo/redo, and persistence.
- Images support add, move, scale, rotate, and delete.
- Owner controls public/hidden state and whether friends may write.
- Owner can share invites, export the book to PDF, and manage/remove participants.
- PageFlip is used for owner browsing of two-page spreads.
- Existing content is preserved; the redesign does not intentionally delete old pages.
- Supabase migration 013 adds editable elements and allocates two-page spreads.
