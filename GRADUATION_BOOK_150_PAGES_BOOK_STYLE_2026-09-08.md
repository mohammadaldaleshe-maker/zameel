# Graduation Book 150-page / realistic book-style upgrade

- Fixed physical capacity: 150 pages / 75 two-page spreads.
- Owner occupies pages 1-2; participants receive the next available odd/even pair.
- Blank pages have no automatic title, student data, or placeholder text.
- Page removal releases a participant's pair without collapsing physical page numbers.
- The Flutter UI renders the spread as a single realistic book with a central gutter/spine, page shadows, and cream paper.
- Left pages are image-only; right pages support handwriting, text, stickers, and images.
- Saving applies deterministic auto-layout while preserving manual content.
