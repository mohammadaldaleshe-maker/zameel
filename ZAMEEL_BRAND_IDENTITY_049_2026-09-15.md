# Zameel Brand Identity 049

**Positioning:** Premium University Social Lifestyle Brand.

## Non-negotiable logo rule

Use the supplied Zameel logo exactly as supplied. Do not redraw or reinterpret the logo. The Z geometry, graduation cap, tassel, proportions and silhouette are permanent. Permitted variants are limited to the supplied full-colour icon and mechanically derived white or official-blue monochrome treatments.

## Official palette

- Royal Blue: `#3152E8`
- Deep Indigo/Violet: `#4B23B7`
- Connection Turquoise: `#27C7C7` (accent only)
- Light Background: `#F7F8FC`
- Deep Night: `#151725`
- Light Surface: `#FFFFFF`
- Dark Surface: `#1D2030`

Target balance: 80% calm neutral surfaces, 15% blue/indigo and 5% turquoise.

## Typography

- English: Inter
- Arabic: IBM Plex Sans Arabic
- Headlines: 700–800; labels: 600–700; body: regular.

## Logo control

- Minimum digital mark: 28 logical pixels.
- Clear space: at least 22% of the displayed mark width on every side.
- Never stretch, rotate, recolour arbitrarily, add effects, place on low-contrast imagery, or separate the cap/tassel from the Z.

## Interface language

- Moderate 14–18 px radii, generous spacing, white/light or deep navy surfaces.
- Clean rounded-outline icons with consistent stroke weight.
- Gradients are reserved for branded moments and primary emphasis.
- Turquoise communicates connection, presence, activity or focus only.
- Photography and student content remain visually dominant.
- Dark mode uses near-black navy rather than pure black.

## Photography and campaigns

Use believable Jordanian and Arab university life, natural clothing and candid connection. People come before screens. Avoid school imagery, stock-photo exaggeration, gaming/fintech aesthetics and excessive glow. Campaigns use one strong photograph, one short phrase and a restrained Zameel mark.

When an image generator is used, begin with:

> Use the attached Zameel logo exactly as supplied. Do not redraw or reinterpret the logo.

For guaranteed accuracy, add the original logo as a locked production layer after generating the surrounding artwork.

## Included application updates

- Supplied master icon installed across Android, iOS, macOS, Windows and Web.
- Exact source geometry used to create official blue and white UI marks.
- Branded Android/iOS launch surfaces.
- Central light and dark theme systems.
- Arabic and English typography selection by app language.
- Existing Zameel 048 behavior, database migration and sonic identity retained.
